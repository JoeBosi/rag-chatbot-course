# Corpus voice notes {#note-vocali-del-corpus}

They are made with a synthetic voice (espeak-ng): they sound robotic
on purpose. They let you run the lab even if you have no microphone
at hand.

Transcribing a synthetic voice is harder than transcribing a real one:
if Whisper gets a few numbers wrong, that is normal and part of the
exercise (see module 7: badly transcribed facts must be corrected, not
left in memory).

Record your own: any phone voice recorder, m4a, wav or mp3 format, and
put them in this folder.
